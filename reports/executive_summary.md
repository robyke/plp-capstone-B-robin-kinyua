# Executive Summary

## PesaPap Customer Growth, Retention and Value Analytics

### Big Idea

PesaPap shows strong overall customer activity, but transaction value is concentrated among a smaller group of High Value customers. Referral is the strongest acquisition channel in the simulated scenario, Send Money is the leading product, and cohort activity generally weakens as customers move further from signup.

## Key Findings

PesaPap has 8,000 registered customers and 7,522 customers completed at least one valid transaction during the observation period, giving an ever-active rate of 94.02%. The cleaned dataset contains 70,824 valid transactions worth KES 111.18 million.

Referral is the strongest acquisition channel by total transaction value, generating approximately KES 29.03 million. Referral customers have a median customer value of KES 13,212.50 compared with KES 11,084.50 for Social customers.

The High Value segment contains 3,221 customers and contributes approximately 68.95% of total transaction value. More than 3,000 customers belong to the Low Activity segment and 478 customers are inactive.

Send Money is the leading product, producing approximately KES 32.98 million from 21,043 transactions.

Cohort analysis shows that activity generally weakens as customers move further from signup. The July 2025 cohort declines from approximately 66.0% active in month 0 to 32.1% by month 11.

## Recommendations

1. Prioritise Referral acquisition while also measuring acquisition cost and scalability.
2. Protect and reward High Value customers.
3. Target Low Activity customers with engagement campaigns.
4. Develop reactivation campaigns for inactive customers.
5. Promote Send Money while testing cross-sell opportunities.
6. Explore high-value counties such as Nyeri, Nakuru and Machakos.
7. Introduce lifecycle retention campaigns several months after signup.

## Expected Business Impact

If applied to real operational data, these actions could improve transaction frequency, customer lifetime value, retention and the efficiency of acquisition spending.

## Limitations

This project uses synthetic data and therefore demonstrates an analytical process rather than actual PesaPap business performance.

The synthetic generator intentionally applies slightly different engagement probabilities by acquisition channel. The Referral versus Social statistical result therefore partly reflects the designed simulation.

The analysis does not contain real acquisition costs, customer profitability, competitor activity or wider economic conditions.

The 94.02% active rate represents customers who completed at least one transaction during the entire observation period and should not be interpreted as current-month active users.
